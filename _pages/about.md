I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the GFZ Helmholtz Centre for Geosciences, working with Deep Learning for Remote Sensing in Forestry Monitoring. Concretely, I'm applying novel and efficient AI approaches to continuous forest disturbance and land monitoring.

> My research interests include Deep Learning, Multi-view Learning, Representation Learning, Model Robustness, and Earth Observation 🌎.

---

Previously, I worked at the German Research Centre for Artificial Intelligence (DFKI) on agriculture- and land-related projects, including crop yield prediction, crop-type mapping, and flood mapping using remote sensing data. I was part of the [Earth & Space Applications team](https://www.ai4eo-factory.de/en/), contributing to research projects and publications in AI for Earth Observation.

I obtained my PhD in Computer Science from the University of Kaiserslautern-Landau (RPTU), Germany, with my doctoral research focusing on *[Multi-view Learning for Earth Observation Applications with Missing Views](https://doi.org/10.26204/KLUEDO/13182)*. My PhD was supervised by [Andreas Dengel](https://www.dfki.uni-kl.de/~dengel/indexEng.php) and mentored by [Diego Arenas](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Diego-Arenas-8).

Before moving to Germany, I obtained my MSc and BSc degrees in Computer Engineering from the Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María (UTFSM), Chile, where I specialized in Artificial Intelligence under the supervision of [Ricardo Ñanculef](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=hndLsckAAAAJ). During my studies, I worked on AI applications across several domains, including crowdsourcing, astroinformatics, and information retrieval.
